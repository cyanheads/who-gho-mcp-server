# 0.3.2 — 2026-08-18

## Fixed

- **`who_query_indicator_data` and `who_list_dimension_values` now fail fast on a lone UTF-16 surrogate in `indicator_code`/`dimension`, instead of burning the full retry budget (~6s) on an unclassified `URIError`.** The failure carries the declared `malformed_identifier` reason and a recovery hint naming the field, on both `structuredContent` and `content[]`. ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/who-gho-mcp-server/issues/18))
